About Tango-7

Your pit stop
in Entebbe

Tango-7 Auto Centre is a car care stop on Bishop Dunstan Road. Bring your car in for a wash, an interior clean, an under-the-hood wash or an oil change.

While the team works, sit in the lounge or order from the restaurant and bar in the same compound.
